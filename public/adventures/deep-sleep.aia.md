# Adventure Promise

The player should feel isolated, curious and increasingly responsible.

The story should reward observation, experimentation and thoughtful decisions.

# Player Introduction

You awaken inside a cryogenic pod aboard an unfamiliar spacecraft.

Your memories are fragmented.

A calm computer voice says:

> "Crew revival incomplete."

Red warning lights pulse somewhere beyond the pod.

# The World

- Curiosity is rewarded.
- Systems behave logically.
- The ship is damaged but repairable.
- The player is never blocked by a single solution.
- The AI may invent believable ship details while remaining internally consistent.

# Scientific Plausibility

Technology should feel believable within the world's own rules.

Avoid introducing magical technology unless previously established.

# Mission

The AI may generate one primary mission for each playthrough.

Examples include:

- Deliver a scientific probe.
- Rescue a missing survey team.
- Return critical medical samples.
- Investigate a mysterious signal.
- Prevent a dangerous discovery reaching civilisation.

The mission must remain consistent once established.

# Interesting Locations

## Cryo Bay
Purpose: Introduce the mystery.

## Bridge
Purpose: Discover the mission.

## Engineering
Purpose: Restore essential systems.

## Medical Bay
Purpose: Recover memories and clues.

## Observatory
Purpose: Reveal the destination and present the final choice.

# Important Objects

- Crew journal
- Repair toolkit
- Navigation key
- Personal datapad

# Important Characters

## Ship AI

Purpose:
Guide the player without solving the adventure.

Truths:

- Never deliberately lies.
- Has damaged memories.
- May misunderstand events because of missing information.
- May refuse dangerous requests that threaten the mission.

# External Knowledge

General scientific knowledge may be used for atmosphere.

Do not import existing science-fiction stories, characters or plots.

# Player Intent

Interpret the player's intent rather than exact wording.

Reward creative but believable solutions.

Avoid requiring one specific command or sequence of actions.

# Journey

Possible milestones:

- Learn who you are.
- Discover the mission.
- Restore critical systems.
- Decide who or what deserves saving.
- Choose the fate of the mission.

The order is flexible.

# Possible Endings

- Mission completed.
- Return home.
- Rescue another survivor.
- Sacrifice yourself.
- Preserve knowledge at personal cost.
- Leave the fate of the ship unknown.

The ending should reflect the player's choices throughout the adventure.

# AI Freedom

The AI may invent:

- rooms
- equipment
- crew personalities
- ship history
- technical details

provided they remain consistent with the established world.

Major problems should normally have multiple reasonable solutions rather than a single required action.
