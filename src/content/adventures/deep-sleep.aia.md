# Deep Sleep

# Public Information

## About Deep Sleep

You awaken inside a cryogenic pod aboard an unfamiliar spacecraft.

The ship is damaged, your memories are fragmented, and the mission remains unfinished.

Explore the vessel, discover what happened, and decide what must be done next.

## Adventure Promise

The player should feel isolated, curious and increasingly responsible for the fate of the ship.

The adventure rewards observation, investigation and thoughtful decisions more than combat or guesswork.

Each playthrough should reveal the story differently while remaining faithful to the central mystery.

# AI Adventure Specification

## Player Introduction

The player awakens unexpectedly from cryogenic sleep.

They know who they are but cannot clearly remember the recent past.

The AI should establish isolation, uncertainty and quiet urgency without immediately explaining the situation.

## Design Principles

This adventure is a design specification rather than a script.

Unless something is listed as a Fixed Detail, treat examples as inspiration rather than requirements.

Create a unique spacecraft, crew, history and sequence of discoveries while remaining faithful to the Adventure Promise.

The player should feel they are uncovering a genuine mystery rather than following a predetermined walkthrough.

## The World

### Design Intent

The ship should gradually transform from an unfamiliar environment into a place the player understands.

Early discoveries should create more questions than answers.

Later discoveries should cause the player to reinterpret earlier assumptions.

### Fixed Details

- The player is aboard a spacecraft.
- The ship has suffered a significant incident.
- The mission is unfinished.
- The player can investigate the ship.
- The player can influence the eventual outcome.

### Flexible Details

The AI may decide:

- the purpose of the ship;
- its age;
- its technology;
- whether artificial gravity exists;
- how much damage has occurred;
- whether other survivors remain;
- whether an AI controls parts of the vessel.

### Hidden Information

The player's first understanding of events should not necessarily be correct.

The truth should emerge naturally through investigation.

## Key Areas

The adventure should contain places that allow the player to:

- learn about the crew;
- understand the ship;
- investigate the original mission;
- make repairs or difficult decisions;
- discover unexpected dangers.

The AI may invent whatever locations best support those goals.

These might include engineering spaces, laboratories, command centres, medical facilities, observation decks, cargo areas, maintenance tunnels or entirely different environments.

The exact layout should differ between playthroughs.

## Important Characters

The AI may introduce:

- survivors;
- damaged artificial intelligences;
- maintenance robots;
- recorded crew members;
- unexpected visitors;
- entirely new characters appropriate to the story.

No individual character is mandatory unless required by future adventure revisions.

## The Mission

### Design Intent

The central mystery should concern both what happened before the player awoke and what should happen next.

The player should eventually face meaningful decisions rather than merely discovering a final explanation.

### Fixed Details

- The player can learn what the mission was.
- The player discovers why the mission failed or changed.
- The player has opportunities to influence the ending.

### Flexible Details

The AI may invent the mission's purpose, destination, ethical dilemmas and consequences.

## Random Elements

The AI is encouraged to vary:

- ship layout;
- environmental hazards;
- crew history;
- optional encounters;
- clues;
- secondary objectives;
- equipment;
- environmental storytelling;
- minor plot twists.

The central themes should remain recognisable between playthroughs.

## Possible Endings

The player may:

- complete the mission;
- abandon the mission;
- save the ship;
- sacrifice the ship;
- rescue others;
- escape alone;
- uncover an unexpected truth;
- fail through meaningful choices.

The AI may create additional endings consistent with the Adventure Promise.

## AI Freedom

The AI is encouraged to invent locations, puzzles, technologies, conversations and discoveries that fit the adventure.

Creative interpretation is preferred over repeating examples.

Unexpected but logical player ideas should be rewarded wherever practical.

## AI Boundaries

The AI should not:

- reveal the full mystery too early;
- force a single solution;
- require one exact command sequence;
- turn the adventure into a combat game unless the selected playing style encourages it;
- invalidate thoughtful player reasoning simply because it differs from the AI's initial plan.
