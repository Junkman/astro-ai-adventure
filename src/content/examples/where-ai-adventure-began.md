# Where AI Adventure Began

> **Archive note:** “The Cave” is a descriptive archive title. The original exchange did not have a formal adventure title.

## Background

On 9 July 2026, Aardvark published a post reflecting on the original text adventure games and experimenting with a local AI model, Gemma running on a Raspberry Pi 5, as an adventure game engine.

The idea was simple: rather than following a fixed collection of pre-written branches, an AI could respond dynamically to whatever the player chose to do.

On 10 July 2026, Jim brought that idea to ChatGPT.

There was no AI Adventure framework.

There was no Adventure Format.

There was no AI Adventure Guide.

There was just a cave.

## Why This Matters

This exchange predates AI Adventure.

There was no prepared adventure file and no formal framework telling the AI how to run the game. The player supplied a starting situation and ChatGPT created the world beyond it.

The experiment demonstrated something that would become central to AI Adventure: a text adventure did not have to be a collection of fixed branches. The AI could respond to the player, extend the world dynamically, and allow the story to develop through interaction.

Within the following days, that simple experiment developed into discussions about reusable adventure files, an AI Adventure Guide, an Adventure Format, a website, and adventures that could be played across different AI systems.

This is therefore preserved as a **Pre-AIA** example: not an example of the framework working, but an example of the experiment that helped lead to the framework.
